Profile

Hands-on production, backed by attention to detail.

I’m Wilberforce Mubiru, a Kampala-based graphic-production and screen-printing professional. My work covers the practical route from artwork preparation to finished output: preparing production graphics, setting and aligning print work, handling colour, monitoring production, and checking that the finished product is clean, readable and consistent.

My portfolio documents screen-printing work from 2020–2022 and later production runs, including apparel, school wear, NGO and campaign materials, institutional workwear, branded merchandise and commercial print jobs. I have practical exposure to screen printing, DTF, sublimation, vinyl cutting and heat-press production, alongside Adobe Illustrator and CorelDRAW for production artwork.

I possess a valid passport and I am ready to travel internationally for work. I am open to remote-site, overseas and deployment-based production roles and prepared to adapt to new operating environments, schedules and team structures.

I value accurate setup, clean registration, consistent colour, dependable output and a workspace that supports safe, repeatable production.
