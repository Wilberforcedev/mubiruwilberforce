Profile

Hands-on production with attention to detail.

I’m Wilberforce Mubiru, a Kampala-based screen-printing and graphic-production professional. My portfolio documents screen-printing work from 2020 to date, including apparel, school wear, NGO and campaign materials, institutional workwear, branded merchandise, bags and commercial print jobs.

My practical production work covers artwork preparation, photo-emulsion screens, registration and alignment, colour mixing, rotary carousel production, press monitoring, curing, heat-press workflows, DTF, sublimation, vinyl and finished-output inspection.

I possess a valid passport and I am ready to travel internationally for work. I am open to remote-site, OCONUS and deployment-based production roles and prepared to adapt to new schedules, procedures, equipment and multi-national team environments.

My production priorities are accurate setup, clean registration, consistent colour, dependable output, safe working practices and an orderly workspace.
