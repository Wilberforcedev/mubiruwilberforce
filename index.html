Frequently asked questions

Common questions about my production work.

Practical answers for recruiters and production teams reviewing my experience.

What production experience do you have?
I have documented screen-printing and press-production work from 2019 to date. The portfolio covers apparel, school wear, NGO and campaign materials, institutional workwear, branded merchandise, bags and commercial print jobs.
Which equipment and processes do you work with?
Rotary carousel screen printing, photo-emulsion screens, printing paste and PVC plastisol colour mixing, flash curing, heat press, DTF, sublimation and vinyl-supported production.
How do you approach quality control?
I check registration and placement, colour consistency, curing and visible defects during the run and before release. Repeat jobs are checked against the approved sample so output stays consistent.
Can you work internationally?
Yes. I hold a valid passport and am ready to travel for work, including remote-site and OCONUS production environments. I am prepared to complete required medicals, visas, credentials and facility authorisation.
What roles are you looking for?
Screen-printing and press-operation roles, plus related production work involving artwork preparation, registration, ink handling, curing and final inspection.
How can we get started?
Contact me on WhatsApp or email with the role, location and production requirements. My CV and production portfolio are available to download, and I am happy to discuss availability.
