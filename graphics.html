Pre-press

Prepare & align

Production begins with artwork that can be printed correctly and screens that are prepared and aligned for the job.

  • Production artwork preparation
  • Colour separation awareness
  • Photo-emulsion screen preparation
  • Screen alignment & registration setup
  • Garment/platen placement checks
Press

Run & monitor

During production I focus on controlled application, alignment and consistency across repeated pieces.

  • Manual/rotary screen-print workflow
  • Ink and colour mixing
  • Registration monitoring
  • Print placement checks
  • Adjustment when output shifts
  • Consistent repeated production
Quality

Inspect & finish

The job is not finished until the print is clean, readable, aligned and properly finished for use.

  • Colour and registration inspection
  • Finished-product visual checks
  • Curing and heat-press finishing
  • Clean handling of garments/materials
  • Orderly production workflow
