Profile

Hands-on production with attention to detail.

I’m Wilberforce Mubiru, a Kampala-based screen-printing and graphic-production professional. My portfolio documents screen-printing work from 2019 to date, including apparel, school wear, NGO and campaign materials, institutional workwear, branded merchandise, bags and commercial print jobs.

My practical production work covers artwork preparation, photo-emulsion screens, registration and alignment, colour mixing, rotary carousel production, press monitoring, curing, heat-press workflows, DTF, sublimation, vinyl and finished-output inspection.

My production profile covers screen setup, ink preparation, material alignment, press operation, print-quality checks, equipment cleaning, supply awareness and production records.

I am open to screen-printing, press-operation and related production opportunities. I am prepared to adapt to different production schedules, procedures, equipment and team environments.

My production priorities are accurate setup, clean registration, consistent colour, dependable output, safe working practices and an orderly workspace.
