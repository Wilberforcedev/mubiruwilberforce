06 · Rogue Ventures

Technology, creative & brand development.

At Rogue Ventures, I work across technology, creative development, and branding, combining my background in Business Computing with my experience in graphic design and digital product development.

My work involves developing and improving digital solutions, creating visual identities and branded materials, and supporting ideas from the early concept stage through to practical implementation. I have worked on web and application development, graphic design, brand identity, digital content, and technology-driven projects, while also contributing ideas around product development and user experience.

I use technologies such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP/Laravel, MySQL, Flutter/Dart, Firebase, and APIs to build and improve digital solutions. On the creative side, I work with Adobe Illustrator and CorelDRAW, with practical experience in logo design, branding, screen printing, DTF, sublimation, vinyl, apparel branding, packaging, and promotional materials.

The role has also given me an opportunity to combine technology with entrepreneurship — understanding a client’s or user’s needs, developing ideas into workable solutions, communicating concepts visually, and working through the practical challenges involved in delivering a project.

About

Through Rogue Ventures I combine Business Computing with hands-on production: logo design and branding, screen printing, DTF, sublimation and vinyl, alongside web and mobile development in Laravel, React and Flutter.
